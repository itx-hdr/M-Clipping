# Current clipping brief

The current niche is **authentic Grand Theft Auto VI player gameplay and reactions to that gameplay**.
All added hooks, captions, titles and descriptions must be in English.

Keep the niche across requests such as “find viral videos” or “make more clips”. Only an explicit user request
changes it. Popularity is a discovery signal, never evidence of the game or of clip quality.

Qualifying footage shows actual player-controlled GTA VI gameplay. A reaction qualifies only when that gameplay
remains visible and understandable. Check sampled footage against primary evidence; a title or thumbnail is not
proof. Official footage described as “captured in-game” may still be a cinematic rather than player gameplay.

Exclude GTA V mods, fan concepts, fake or uncertain GTA VI claims, trailer-only reactions, news/speculation,
and unrelated games. If no verified gameplay is available, report that result and keep searching within the niche.
Do not substitute other games or old trailer reactions to fill a requested count.

The MCP workflow is: brief → discovery → footage inspection → source review → queue → rendered video inspection
and audio check → repair → final review → export. Source and final reviews are agent assessments with recorded
evidence. They are not automated proof of authenticity or promises of views.

Export only a complete short: truthful opening hook, clear context, visible payoff, natural ending, readable
English captions, useful vertical framing and clean audio. Keep review history when a cut or model changes;
do not keep regrading an unchanged cut to obtain a higher number. Preserve the actual scores and reasons.

The initial MCP gate is 85/100, at least 17/20 in each dimension, no unresolved issues, no missing review checks,
and a successful media check.
An unavailable grader or an uncertain niche match means the clip remains a draft.
